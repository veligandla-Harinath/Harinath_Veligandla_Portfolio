Ecommerce Sales Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project analyzing 10,194 retail sales records from the Superstore dataset. The workflow covers cleaning and preparing raw data in Python (Pandas, NumPy), querying it in MySQL to answer business questions around sales, profit, and discounts, and visualizing the results in an interactive Power BI dashboard. The dashboard tracks key metrics — $2.33M in total sales, $292K in profit, and 39K units sold — broken down by category, region, segment, product, and customer, with slicers for dynamic filtering and a monthly trend view from 2023 to 2026. Key finding: Technology is the top-performing category in both sales and profit, while Furniture generates high sales but disproportionately low profit, pointing to a pricing and discount strategy that needs review.
